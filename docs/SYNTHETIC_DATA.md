# Synthetic generation

Seed: `20251001`. Generator version: `1.0`. Observation: **2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30**,
inclusive. No current-clock input, network lookup or real personal information is
used. Generation uses an isolated `random.Random` instance and explicit calendar
arithmetic. All records carry `synthetic=true`, explicit fixed UTC creation times,
and deterministic assigned integer IDs. Documents also state their fictional status.

The generator first creates master data and commercial commitments, then simulates
each day: incoming receipts, new sales demand, FIFO outstanding lines and physical
dispatch. All inbound movements precede issues on the same day. Each event records
its own source and quantity. There is no invented opening balance. Partial supplier
deliveries arrive three days apart; future receipt events stay outside the dataset.
Open commitments and unpaid invoices remain visible at the cutoff.

Supplier IDs congruent to 1 modulo 6 have a 60% delayed first-receipt probability;
other suppliers have 12%, with randomized lead times and noise. The former have a
0.90 price multiplier versus 1.08 for the more reliable group. These are generator
assumptions, not scores or API conclusions. November/December customer demand has
a 1.30 quantity multiplier versus 0.85 elsewhere. Demand can exceed individual
replenishment quantities and use stock from earlier receipts. Midlands capacity
holds add a separate operational cause of delays.

Product 1 is isolated for an independently checkable procurement-to-customer case:
40 units promised 8 October arrive 20 October; 30 units ordered 9 October and
promised 10 October dispatch on 20 October. Every other product participates in
the noisy multi-warehouse simulation. Case IDs are not returned as analytical
answers by the API; verification queries inspect the underlying records.

Incident closure follows actual full receipt or full dispatch. Invoice disputes and
capacity escalations remain unresolved. Corrective tasks start open and retain
their resulting status in history; completed tasks have matching completion evidence.
Finance/warehouse incidents also route workload into Operations.

Supplier agreement text is generated from contract rows; eight distinct operating
policies bring the corpus to 44 documents. SHA-256 values detect corpus drift before
seeding. To intentionally regenerate the corpus after changing generator terms:

```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH = 'backend;.'
uv run python -c "from pathlib import Path; from data.generator.company import generate; generate().write_documents(Path('.'))"
```

Review changed documents before reset/reseed. Ordinary seeding never overwrites them.

The logical fingerprint includes every non-seed table and document's contents,
sorted mapping keys, row IDs, fixed timestamps and canonical two-decimal money.
`seed_runs` stores the fingerprint, seed and version; it is excluded from its own
digest. PostgreSQL-loaded data must reproduce the same fingerprint. IDs here are
assigned deterministically; future autogenerated IDs or wall-clock metadata would
need explicit normalization for logical comparisons. Do not silently omit business
fields to make mismatched fingerprints pass.
