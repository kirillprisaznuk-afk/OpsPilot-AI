## Interview introduction

I built OpsPilot to connect a business finding to the evidence and the people accountable for acting on it. I can open a supplier’s delivery results, compare them with its agreement, then propose an internal review that another person has to approve. The product records the decision, the execution and its outcome.

## Engineering explanation

The Next.js gateway controls browser sessions and proxies typed requests to FastAPI. PostgreSQL provides scoped analytical reads, a pgvector corpus and authoritative workflow state. The interpreter builds a bounded read-only plan and verifies source citations. The write path rechecks role, version, digest and preconditions and uses transaction locks and idempotency. The local model adapter remains an experiment and is outside the demonstrated workflow path.
